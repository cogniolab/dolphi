# Code of conduct

Dolphi welcomes people with different backgrounds, experience and perspectives.
Help make participation useful, respectful and safe.

- Discuss ideas and code with specific, constructive feedback.
- Respect privacy and confidentiality; use synthetic data in examples.
- Credit others' work and follow applicable project licenses.
- Do not harass, discriminate, threaten, impersonate others or publish private
  information. Avoid sexualized content and repeated disruptive behavior.
- Respect requests to stop and follow reasonable maintainer moderation.

This applies to project issues, pull requests, discussions and project-related
interactions. Maintainers may edit/remove inappropriate content or restrict
participation, considering impact, repetition and context.

Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer contact specified in
[SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md). Include relevant links and the minimum necessary
context; do not post sensitive reports publicly. Maintainers should protect
reporter privacy, consider conflicts and explain moderation decisions when safe.
The private contact must be established before public launch.
